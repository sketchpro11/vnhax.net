about vnhax

We make technology easier to understand. vnhax brings together the tech news that matters, practical AI insights, and the developer resources we wish existed — in one clean place.

Our Mission

Technology moves fast, and keeping up shouldn't feel like a second job. Our mission is simple: filter the noise, explain what actually matters, and give developers and learners free, high-quality resources they can use right away. Everything on vnhax is free to read, and our UI components and code resources are free to use.

What You'll Find Here

  • Tech news and innovation — clear, concise coverage of the products, platforms, and companies shaping the industry.
  • AI insights — practical explainers and analysis of artificial intelligence tools, models, and trends, written for real people rather than press releases.
  • Education guides — step-by-step tutorials and reference material that help you learn by doing.
  • Free UI components — clean, dependency-free interface building blocks you can drop into your own projects.
  • Developer designs — layout inspiration and design resources built with modern front-end standards.
  • Top GitHub repositories — hand-picked, actively maintained open-source projects worth your time.

How We Work

We are an independent, reader-supported publication. Our editorial choices are not for sale: advertisers and sponsors never influence what we cover or how we cover it. Every guide is tested before publication, every repository we feature is reviewed for maintenance and license clarity, and every claim links back to its source.

When we make a mistake, we correct it visibly. When a story is an opinion rather than reporting, we label it as such. If something here saves you an hour of searching, the site is doing its job.

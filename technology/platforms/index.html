Technology & platforms

platform guides

A platform is more than a feature checklist. The useful question is whether it can carry your work safely, predictably, and without creating a maintenance burden your team cannot afford.

Five questions before adopting a platform

  1. Does it fit the current architecture? Map the required integrations, data flows, and identity boundaries before a proof of concept becomes a production dependency.
  2. Can you change course? Understand data export, API portability, contracts, and the work required to leave if the product changes.
  3. Who will operate it? Account for monitoring, on-call ownership, upgrades, access reviews, and support escalation—not only initial setup.
  4. What enters the trust boundary? Review permissions, retention, regional processing, audit trails, and the vendor’s security documentation for the data you will send.
  5. What does success look like? Define a measurable outcome and a limited trial period before committing budget or moving a critical workflow.

Use these questions to structure vendor evaluations, open-source pilots, and internal platform proposals. The point is not to eliminate uncertainty; it is to make the remaining tradeoffs visible.

> **Quick answer (checked 12 October 2026):** Google released the official **Gemini app for Windows** on **10 September 2026**, available **globally for Windows 10 and 11**. Download it only from **[gemini.google/desktop](https://gemini.google/desktop/)**, install it, sign in with your Google account, then press **Alt + Space** from any app to open Gemini over your work. The shortcut can be changed in the app's settings. The app is free to use with a Google account; Google says "selected features" need a Google AI subscription.

Before September 2026, "Gemini on Windows" meant a browser tab or a third-party wrapper. Now there is an official desktop app from Google. Here is how to get it safely and what it actually does, based on Google's announcement and download page (sources at the end).

## What the Gemini Windows app does

Google's launch post highlights three things:

1. **A keyboard shortcut from anywhere:** press **Alt + Space** to open Gemini on top of whatever you're working on, ask something, and go straight back to your work.
2. **A full workspace:** everything you use Gemini for on the web, plus **Gemini Spark**, which Google calls your 24/7 personal AI agent for multi-step tasks, and answers that pull from your **Gmail, Google Drive, Docs and Calendar**.
3. **Image and video creation:** images with **Nano Banana** and videos with **Gemini Omni**, from the desktop.

Google's download page also mentions sharing your screen so Gemini can help debug errors or explain charts, using Gemini Spark to tidy folders or summarize local files into Docs and Sheets, and dictation that formats your speech in any app. Google says some of these features are on macOS first and will come to Windows over time.

## Requirements

- **Windows 10 or Windows 11** (Google's launch post).
- A **Google account** and an internet connection: Gemini runs in Google's cloud, not on your PC's GPU.
- Some features are limited to users **18+ with a Google AI subscription**, according to the download page.

Google hasn't published RAM or disk requirements. It describes the app as "lightweight and quiet".

## How to install it

1. **Go to the official page:** open **[gemini.google/desktop](https://gemini.google/desktop/)** in your browser. Don't download "Gemini for PC" installers from other sites; fake AI app installers are a common way to spread malware.
2. **Download for Windows:** click **Download for Windows** and save the installer.
3. **Run the installer:** open the downloaded file and follow the prompts. If Windows asks for permission, check that the publisher shown is Google before you accept.
4. **Sign in** with your Google account. If you use a work or school account, your organization's admin may control whether Gemini is available to you.
5. **Try the shortcut:** switch to another app (a browser, Word, VS Code) and press **Alt + Space**. Gemini should open over it.

On a Mac, the same app uses **Option + Space**.

## Changing the Alt + Space shortcut

Alt + Space has a long history on Windows: it opens the classic window menu (Restore, Minimize, Close), and tools such as PowerToys Run have used it too. If the shortcut doesn't open Gemini, or you'd rather keep the old behaviour, change Gemini's shortcut in the **Gemini app settings**. Google's page says the shortcut can be customized at any time.

## Free vs paid

You can use the app with a free Google account. Google's footnote says **selected features are available with a Google AI subscription** for users 18 and over, but doesn't list exactly which ones. In practice, higher usage limits and the most advanced models and agent features are where subscriptions usually come in, so check the plan details in your account if a feature is missing.

## Troubleshooting

- **Alt + Space does nothing:** another app is probably using the same shortcut. Close or reconfigure it, or pick a different shortcut in Gemini's settings.
- **"Not available in your country":** Google's FAQ on the download page covers regional availability. The app launched globally, but individual features can vary by country and account.
- **Can't sign in with a work account:** ask your IT admin whether Gemini is enabled for your Google Workspace organization.
- **A feature from this guide is missing:** Google is rolling out desktop features over time, and some arrived on macOS first.


## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Gemini app for Windows free?
Yes, you can use it with a free Google account. Some features need a Google AI subscription.

### Does it work on Windows 10?
Yes. Google says the app is available globally for Windows 10 and 11.

### Does Gemini run on my PC's graphics card?
No. The app sends your requests to Google's servers; it doesn't run the model locally.

### Where is the official download?
Only at gemini.google/desktop.

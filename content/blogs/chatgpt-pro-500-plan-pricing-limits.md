> **Executive summary:** At OpenAI DevDay on 29 September 2026, Sam Altman introduced the **ChatGPT Pro 500** subscription tier alongside key structural revisions to the existing $200/month Pro plan. New sign-ups for the $200 tier receive 10x the usage limits of ChatGPT Plus, while Pro 500 and Enterprise subscribers gain exclusive access to the **Ultrafast** speed tier on GPT-6 Astra.

OpenAI’s DevDay 2026 brought sweeping updates to its consumer and enterprise subscription architecture. In response to surging demand for heavy compute reasoning models like GPT-6 Astra and o3, OpenAI restructured its premium subscriptions, creating a tiered hierarchy for power users, autonomous agent operators, and enterprise engineering teams.

Here is an objective overview of the ChatGPT Pro 500 plan, the modifications to the $200 tier, and how the changes affect current and prospective subscribers.

## What Is ChatGPT Pro 500?

Announced on stage by Sam Altman, **ChatGPT Pro 500** is a specialized subscription designed for developers and researchers whose daily workloads consistently hit rate ceilings on standard plans. 

Key attributes of the Pro 500 tier include:
- **Maximized Usage Caps:** Significantly elevated token throughput and concurrency thresholds compared to standard Pro accounts.
- **Dedicated Compute Priority:** Access to the **Ultrafast** inference tier for GPT-6 Astra, dramatically lowering Time-to-First-Token (TTFT) and token generation latency during peak worldwide usage hours.
- **Priority Agent Provisioning:** Native priority queueing for background execution tasks running on OpenAI dots.

## What Changed for the $200 Pro Plan?

Following the DevDay announcements, OpenAI product lead Thibault Sottiaux clarified several key adjustments regarding the existing $200/month Pro plan:

### 1. Updated Usage Multipliers
New subscribers joining the $200 Pro tier will now receive **10x the usage limits of ChatGPT Plus**, revised from the previous 20x multiplier. This recalibration reflects the massive compute density demands of next-generation frontier reasoning models.

### 2. Grandfathered Protections for Existing Subscribers
Existing $200 Pro subscribers maintain their grandfathered **20x usage allocation** for an unspecified transitional window. This ensures long-standing power users who relied on the previous caps do not encounter immediate operational disruption.

### 3. Reopened Sign-Ups
OpenAI formally reopened public subscriptions for the $200 Pro plan following a temporary freeze instituted on 10 September 2026 due to compute constraints.

## Pro 500 and the Ultrafast Speed Tier

A central distinction of the Pro 500 tier is early access to OpenAI’s **Ultrafast** inference pipeline. Operating on dedicated speculative decoding clusters and optimized KV cache architectures, the Ultrafast tier delivers up to 3x higher token generation velocities on complex coding and multi-step reasoning tasks.

For teams deploying high-frequency autonomous scripts or interactive pair-programming sessions, this speed differential translates into tangible operational productivity.

## Who Should Upgrade?

1. **Keep Plus ($20/mo):** If your primary use case involves document drafting, exploratory queries, and standard coding assistance where standard rate limits are rarely reached.
2. **Choose Pro ($200/mo):** If you run continuous coding agent sessions, need multi-hour background reasoning, and regularly exhaust Plus caps.
3. **Upgrade to Pro 500:** If you operate demanding autonomous agent pipelines via OpenAI dots, require sub-second generation latency via the Ultrafast tier, or run mission-critical production evaluations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the $200 Pro plan being cancelled?
No. The $200 Pro plan remains fully active and has reopened to new subscribers with an updated 10x usage multiplier over Plus.

### Do existing $200 Pro subscribers lose their 20x limit?
Existing subscribers preserve their 20x multiplier for an unspecified transitional period.

### What is the primary benefit of ChatGPT Pro 500 over the $200 plan?
ChatGPT Pro 500 provides higher concurrent usage caps and access to the low-latency Ultrafast inference tier for GPT-6 Astra.

## Conclusion

The introduction of ChatGPT Pro 500 and the recalibration of the $200 tier illustrate the scaling dynamics of frontier generative models.

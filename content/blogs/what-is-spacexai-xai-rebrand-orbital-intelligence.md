> **Quick answer (checked 8 November 2026):** **xAI**, the company behind Grok, was acquired by SpaceX in **February 2026** and rebranded as **SpaceXAI** in **July 2026**. On **4 October 2026** Elon Musk said it would become **SpaceXSI** ("SI" for super intelligence), and the switch has since happened in practice: the X account is now **@SpaceXSI**, and x.ai's developer docs and blog posts are signed SpaceXSI, with the site footer reading "SpaceX Super Intelligence LLC". For Grok users and API developers, almost nothing practical has changed: the same models, the same `docs.x.ai` documentation and the same API.

If you have seen "xAI", "SpaceXAI" and "SpaceXSI" in the space of a few weeks, you are not imagining things. Here is the short version of how we got here, based on the sources linked at the end.

## Timeline: from xAI to SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI

| Date | What happened |
|---|---|
| March 2023 | Elon Musk founds **xAI**. Grok 1 launches that November. |
| March 2025 | xAI is combined with X Corp. (the X social network) under X.AI Holdings. |
| February 2026 | **SpaceX acquires xAI**, at a reported valuation of $250 billion. |
| July 2026 | xAI is **rebranded as SpaceXAI**, with a new logo; Grok products now operate under the SpaceXAI name. |
| August 2026 | SpaceX acquires the AI coding company **Cursor**, which now sits under SpaceXAI. |
| 4 October 2026 | Musk says on X that SpaceXAI **will be renamed SpaceXSI**. |
| ~10 October 2026 | The company's X account changes from @SpaceXAI to **@SpaceXSI** ("SpaceX Super Intelligence"). |
| October–November 2026 | Announcements on x.ai, such as Grok Voice 3.0 on 14 October, are signed **SpaceXSI**; by early November the site footer reads "SpaceX Super Intelligence LLC". |

Today the company's main products are the **Grok** family of language, image and video models, **Grok Build** and **Grok Bot**, plus the **X** social network and **Cursor** through subsidiaries. It also runs the **Colossus** AI data centers in Memphis.

## Why "SpaceXSI"?

Musk posted "No more AI" and "SI… It's better", and replied "Yes, we will make that change" when a user suggested renaming SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI. Coverage linked the timing to a 29 September US executive order that asks federal agencies to use "Super Intelligence" and "SI" instead of "AI" in official communications.

There was **no formal press release or effective date**. Instead the new name appeared step by step: first the developer docs title ("SpaceXSI Docs", seen on 12 October), then the X account, then announcements and the site footer. The domains are still `x.ai` and `docs.x.ai`.

## What it means for Grok users and developers

- **Your API code doesn't change.** The API, keys and model IDs such as `grok-4.7` stay the same, and documentation still lives at [docs.x.ai](https://docs.x.ai/developers/models).
- **Grok in X and the Grok apps works as before.** The rename is about the company, not the products.
- **Expect mixed names in documents.** Invoices, terms and dashboards may show xAI, SpaceXAI or SpaceXSI for a while. Check the exact company name on contracts if your procurement team cares about it.
- **The roadmap is about compute.** Musk has said the company plans to build data centers in space. That is a long-term ambition, not something announced with dates, so I would not plan around it.

## Is SpaceXSI the same company as SpaceX?

Not quite. SpaceXSI (formerly SpaceXAI, and before that xAI) is a **subsidiary** of SpaceX, the rocket and Starlink company. SpaceX still runs its launch business separately; SpaceXSI is its AI division.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is xAI shutting down?
No. xAI was renamed SpaceXAI after SpaceX acquired it. The people, products and API continue under the new name.

### Has SpaceXAI become SpaceXSI?
In practice, yes. There was no formal announcement, but by mid-October the X account was @SpaceXSI, and x.ai announcements and docs now use SpaceXSI, with the footer reading "SpaceX Super Intelligence LLC".

### Do I need to change my Grok API integration?
No. Endpoints, keys and model IDs are unchanged.

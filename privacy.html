1. Overview

vnhax ("we", "our", or "the site") is an independent publication covering technology news, AI trends, education guides, free UI components, developer design resources, and curated GitHub repositories. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit vnhax, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide voluntarily. We do not require accounts or registration. The only details you may share with us directly are:

  • Your email address, if you subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Your name, email address, and message contents, if you contact us through our contact form or by email.

Information collected automatically. When you browse the site, standard log data is recorded, which may include your IP address, browser type and version, device type, referring page, pages visited, and timestamps. We also use cookies and similar technologies as described below.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember preferences. vnhax uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand aggregate traffic and improve our content.
  • Advertising cookies — used by our advertising partners, including Google, to serve and measure ads (see Section 4).

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Vendors

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements that keep vnhax free to read. In doing so:

  • Google uses cookies, including the DART cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party cookies used for advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

Advertising partners may use device identifiers, location (coarse), and browsing data to serve relevant ads. We do not control, and are not responsible for, the data practices of third-party ad vendors. Please review their respective privacy policies for details.

EU/UK users: where required by Google's EU User Consent Policy and the GDPR, we present a consent request before non-essential cookies are set, and ads are not personalized unless you consent.

5. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure traffic and understand how visitors interact with our content. Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, time on page, device category, and approximate geographic region. It does not give us access to your name, exact location, or other directly identifying information. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the official browser add-on.

6. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site and its content.
  • To send our newsletter if you subscribed (every email includes an unsubscribe link).
  • To respond to inquiries, feedback, or support requests.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or security issues.
  • To measure traffic and analyze content performance in aggregate.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share limited data only with service providers who help us operate the site (such as email delivery and analytics providers), under contracts that restrict how they may use it, or when required by law, regulation, or legal process.

8. Data Security and Retention

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. Newsletter emails are stored until you unsubscribe. Contact messages are retained only as long as needed to handle your request. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your data (e.g., remove your email from our newsletter list).
  • Objection and restriction — object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Non-discrimination — we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

California residents: we do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA. To exercise any right, email us at hello@vnhax.net and we will respond within the legally required timeframe.

10. Children's Privacy

vnhax is not directed at children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
